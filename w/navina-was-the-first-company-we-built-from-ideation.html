Navina was the first company we built from ideation

A journey worth celebrating!

From day one, I’ve had the privilege of supporting the extraordinary team at Navina as an investor and partner. They were the first company I guided through the entire ideation process—proving a model we’ve successfully replicated since. Watching them evolve from an idea on paper to a pioneering force in primary healthcare has been nothing short of inspiring.

In the latest ▶️ #Meet_the_Founder episode, Ronen Lavi (CEO & Co-Founder) shares how their cutting-edge AI is turning fragmented patient data into a clear, actionable patient portrait—empowering physicians to deliver exceptional care. This is precisely the kind of impact that makes us proud to be part of their journey.
