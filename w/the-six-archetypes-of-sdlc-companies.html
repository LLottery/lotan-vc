The six archetypes of SDLC companies

We have just published the second part of Grove Ventures "Shift Happens" Report: The 6 Archetypes of Companies that Focus on the Software Development Life Cycle

It's our new simplified framework to categorize SDLC-focused startups into six distinct archetypes, based on their Go-to-Market strategies.

Read about 'The Founding Fathers' archetype, the innovative 'DevFlix & Chill' companies, and the strategic 'Magicians,' to learn about how this framework provides a fresh lens to understanding the SDLC landscape.

Identifying the appropriate archetype can assist companies in shaping their go-to-market strategies effectively, accelerating their growth, and proactively addressing unique challenges ahead.
