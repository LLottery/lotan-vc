Laziness is a feature

Laziness is a key to innovation. Not a bug. A feature.

Every breakthrough started because someone simply did not want to make the effort. Too lazy to walk? We invented the car. Too lazy to write a letter? We invented the telephone. Too lazy to write a design doc before code? We invented low-code and LLMs.

Instead of fighting laziness, listen to it. It points precisely at the places that need automation, intuitiveness, or a paradigm shift.

Whoever complains about the shortcuts of the new generation forgets that everything they use today started as the previous generation's shortcut.

Innovation is the best justification for laziness. And laziness is often the best trigger for innovation.

Real founders identify laziness and turn it into a startup. What is the next thing that needs solving simply because you are tired of the effort?
