Software infrastructure belongs to those who harness AI

Shift Happens 3.0 is almost here! 🚀 This Thursday marks Grove Ventures third consecutive year hosting this event, and every edition has shown just how quickly innovation shifts in the world of software infrastructure. This year, we’re taking it further: diving deep into how the next wave of Gen AI is reshaping the foundations of technology itself.
