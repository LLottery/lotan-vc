Partnering with Navina before it was an idea

Excited to celebrate Navina's $55M Series C round led by Goldman Sachs Asset Management ! Over six years ago, I partnered with Ronen Lavi and Shay Perera before Navina was even an idea. Watching their journey—from envisioning AI in the hands of every doctor long before it was obvious, to now impacting over 10,000 clinicians and 3 million patients—is nothing short of inspiring. Great to continue the journey alongside Vertex Ventures Israel, ALIVE Israel HealthTech Fund, Schusterman Family Investments (SFI), and JSL Health Capital. Congrats Ronen and Shay—excited to keep supporting Navina’s mission to transform healthcare. Welcome Irit Kahan!
