Service as Software 2025-10-23

Trust is the key

Something big is happening at the seam between service and software. This week we tried to understand what it looks like up close, at a meetup Grove Ventures held with Google Cloud on Service as Software.

The bottom line? We are still very early in this journey. There are more question marks than answers, but one thing is clear: the opportunity is real.

The way we built and sold SaaS products will not be able to replace service providers without deep changes. To turn a service into a product, companies will need to walk Palantir's path: add organizational innovation and GTM innovation to the product innovation. And to redefine how software works in a world where everything runs on trust.

The key is trust. Every startup in this space has to build a credible brand that can stand in front of enterprise customers. And there is a lot to learn from the giants that have ruled these worlds for decades.

From the founders on the panel, it was fascinating to see how differently each approaches the challenge, and yet all of them already feel strong signals from the market that they are pointed in the right direction.

It also looks like a big opportunity is opening here for people who combine a technical background with business understanding and the ability to work with customers. Every one of these companies is growing and hiring. If you are a developer curious about business, product, or the customer side, this is an excellent time to start moving in that direction.
