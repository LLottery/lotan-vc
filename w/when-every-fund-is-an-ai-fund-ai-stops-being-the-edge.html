When every fund is an AI fund, AI stops being the edge

In Berlin this week. On Wednesday I'm on a panel at Super Venture that I think is the real debate right now: Alpha or beta? Rethinking venture strategy in the age of AI.

My starting position: when every fund is an AI fund, AI itself stops being the edge. So where is the alpha now?

After a decade investing in deep tech and AI across 50+ companies, that's the question I most want to dig into on stage.

I'll be joining Charles Schrager (Isomer Capital), Xiaolei Cong (Innovius), Julien-David Nitlech (Iris Capital), and Jenny Xiao (Leonis Capital) to get into it. If you'll be in Berlin, I'd love to connect. The panel is Wednesday, June 10, at 11:35 AM. #SuperVenture #VentureCapital #AI
